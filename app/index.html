Installing

  1. Tap Download. If the browser warns the file may be harmful, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the file. Android asks once to allow installs from the browser: Settings → Allow from this source, back, Install. An older MSTAT is updated in place; your runs and methods are kept.
  3. Open MSTAT, allow Bluetooth.
  4. Lab tablets: Device → Firmware update → Beta (lab).
  5. Connect a board: the PIN is on its sticker.

Never connect USB to a V1.2 board: battery only.
